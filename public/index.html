How to Play

Welcome to the Squares in Squares solver engine.

The Goal: Pack N unit squares (side length = 1) into the smallest possible larger square container without overlapping. Most optimal configurations are still unknown to mathematics! (Read more at squaremath.org)

Controls

  • Select: Click a square on the grid, or click its row in the table. Use TAB or SHIFT+TAB to cycle through squares.
  • Type Select: When focused on the grid, simply type a square's number to jump to it.
  • Move (Mouse): Click and drag a selected square across the grid.
  • Move (Keyboard): Use W, A, S, D or the Arrow Keys to nudge the selected square up, left, down, or right.
  • Rotate (Keyboard): Use Q to rotate counter-clockwise, and E to rotate clockwise.
  • Micro-Adjustments: Hold down the Shift key while pressing any keyboard control to make 10x smaller, precise adjustments.
  • Collision Constraints (CTRL): Hold down CTRL while moving or dragging. The engine will prevent the square from causing an overlap or increasing the bounding box.
  • Undo/Redo: Use Ctrl+Z and Ctrl+Y (or the buttons on the canvas).
